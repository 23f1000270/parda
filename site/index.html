What happens to your picture

Two small neural networks run inside this tab: YuNet finds faces and a YOLOv9 model finds number plates. Each one is drawn over with a coarse grid of averaged colour plus random noise. Plain blur can be partly reversed by AI tools; a six-cell grid leaves nothing to recover.

When a face or plate disappears for a moment, Parda keeps covering where it was for a few more frames and makes the cover a little bigger each time. If a model crashes, the whole frame goes dark rather than showing anything unmasked.

Saved videos have no sound. This page sends no picture data anywhere: open your browser's network panel and check.

Photos get a full scan: the whole picture, then full-resolution tiles to catch small faces in crowds and distant plates. Camera and video use one fast pass per frame so they keep up. For long videos, the command-line tool does the full scan on every frame.

Models: YuNet (OpenCV Zoo, MIT) and YOLOv9-t plate detector (open-image-models, MIT). Parda is Apache-2.0.
